Record Hebrew for Tyler

← Back to Hebrew Cards

286 cards in the same order as the game. This is a practical learning set, not a list of words every child must know by a particular age.

Use Voice Memos in a quiet room. Record batches of 20–30 items. For each item, say its number in English, pause, say only the Hebrew word or phrase naturally, then leave about two seconds of silence. Name the recording with its number range, for example “Hebrew 001–020”. Send the audio files in chat so they can be split and matched to cards. If you make a mistake, say “again”, repeat the number, and read the Hebrew again.

Masculine and feminine meanings are noted where needed. Infinitives are used for the action cards. Read the Hebrew, not the English cue. Finished clips added to this public website will be available to anyone who visits it.
